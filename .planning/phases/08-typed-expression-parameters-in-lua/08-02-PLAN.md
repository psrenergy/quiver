  expression.cpp's included headers (sol2's and Quiver's), so a genuine unreachable-code warning from those headers
  inside this one TU would be hidden; the other 16 TUs still report it. The scope is the narrowest the research found
  that works without relying on a later reader not adding a never-returning attribute.

  Phase 7 run-to-run spread was about plus or minus 10% (1857-2255 ms), so the medians of five interleaved runs in one
  session are compared, never numbers from different sessions.

  `__index` pinned by a test, no per-state registration). The ROADMAP wording "used if the tag costs measurably" then
  names a condition that did not occur; the SUMMARY records this as the plan's decision, not a gap.

ordering as explicit truths, and LUA-07 as FA-5. 08-01 authors the other 17 (see its accounting): 22 = 16 explicit + 3
